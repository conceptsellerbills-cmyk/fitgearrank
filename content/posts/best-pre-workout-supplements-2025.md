# Best Pre-Workout Supplements 2025: Top 7 for Energy, Focus & Pump

Pre-workout supplements can meaningfully improve training performance — but the market is flooded with underdosed, overpriced, proprietary-blend products that deliver little more than a caffeine rush and a tingling face.

We tested 15+ pre-workouts across multiple training sessions, evaluating energy, focus, endurance, pump, and crash. Here are the 7 best pre-workouts of 2025.

## What Makes a Good Pre-Workout?

**Evidence-based ingredients at effective doses:**
- Caffeine: 150-300mg for energy and focus
- Beta-alanine: 3.2g for muscular endurance (causes the tingle)
- Citrulline malate: 6-8g for pump and blood flow
- Creatine monohydrate: 3-5g for strength and power
- Betaine anhydrous: 2.5g for power output

Avoid proprietary blends — they hide ingredient doses.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should I take pre-workout?
20-30 minutes before training for most formulas. Caffeine peaks in the bloodstream at 45-60 minutes.

### Is pre-workout safe?
At recommended doses, yes. Avoid if you have heart conditions, anxiety, or caffeine sensitivity. Don't exceed one serving per day.

### Do I need pre-workout?
No. Black coffee (100mg caffeine per cup) provides most of the ergogenic benefit at a fraction of the cost. Pre-workouts add beta-alanine, citrulline, and creatine that coffee doesn't.
